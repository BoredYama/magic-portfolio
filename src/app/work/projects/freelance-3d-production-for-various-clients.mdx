From May 2024 to Oct 2025, I worked as a freelance 3D Artist for various clients, supporting short-turnaround production and quality-focused deliveries.

- Executed UV unwrapping and texture workflows for realistic, consistent output.
- Delivered assets optimized for animation and product presentation use cases.

- Autodesk Maya
- Substance Painter
- ZBrush
- Blender 3D

## Outcome

Maintained reliable delivery quality across varied project requirements and timelines while adapting to different client briefs.
