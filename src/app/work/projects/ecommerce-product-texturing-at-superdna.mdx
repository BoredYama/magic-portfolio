## Overview

At SuperDNA 3D Lab, I worked as a Freelance 3D Artist from Aug 2022 to Aug 2023, building product-focused assets for eCommerce use cases.

## Responsibilities

- Broke down product reference images into texturing tasks.
- Executed UV unwrapping for clean texture application.
- Produced realistic textures to match references as closely as possible.

## Tools and Workflow

- Autodesk Maya
- Substance Painter
- Substance Designer
- UV and texturing workflows for production consistency

## Outcome

Delivered production-ready product assets that supported realistic visualization needs for eCommerce clients.
