## Overview

Since Jan 2025, I have been working as a Quality Analyst at Assemble / Clickable Network, a YouTube channel, where I review videos for publishing quality and accuracy.

## Responsibilities

- Identifies visual or audio errors in video content before release.
- Provides corrected transcripts for the videos.
- Writes detailed, actionable feedback for artists in Google Sheets.

## Tools and Workflow

- Frame-by-frame visual and audio review
- Transcript verification and correction
- Feedback tracking through Google Sheets

## Outcome

Improved publishing quality and consistency by catching issues early, correcting transcripts, and maintaining clear feedback records for the production team.
