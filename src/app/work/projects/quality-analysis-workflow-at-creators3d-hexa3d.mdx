## Overview

As a Quality Analyst at Creators3D / Hexa3D, I focused on improving delivery quality for 3D assets through structured review and feedback.

## Responsibilities

- Compared 3D models to client-provided reference images.
- Evaluated deliverables against internal and client quality guidelines.
- Wrote detailed, practical feedback to help artists improve asset quality.

## Tools and Workflow

- Image-based quality review process
- Documentation-oriented feedback loops
- Cross-team collaboration with artists

## Outcome

Improved review clarity and consistency by translating quality standards into actionable feedback for production teams.
